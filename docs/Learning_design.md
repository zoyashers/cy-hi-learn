CY-HI Learning - Learning Design

learning philosophy

CY-HI is designed around the idea that cybersecurity students need more than theoretical knowledge.

The platform focuses on developing the ability to:

Understand evidence
Ask useful questions
Form hypotheses
Test hypotheses
Interpret findings
Avoid unsupported conclusions communicate findings clearly

The goal is to develop investigation habits rather than simply memorising cybersecurity tools.

Learning loop

The core learning loop is:

Learn - Practice - Investigate - Receive feedback - Reflect = Progress

Students can therefore move repeatedly between theory and practical application.

Learning units - Provide the structured educational content required to understand cybersecurity
                 topic. It can also provide the knowledge required before a student attempts
                related practical activities.

Missions - Practical scenario-based activities 

Cases - Represent broader investigative contexts .Containing multiple pieces of evidence and allowing
        students to approach cybersecurity problems as investigations rather than isolated exercises.

Difficulty - Missions support different difficutly levels. For example easy gets 4 hints while medium
             gets 3 and hard gets 2.The intentions is to reqard students who can solve increasingly 
             difficult problems with less support.

Progression - students receive XP through practical learning activity:

XP contributes to:

Levels
Ranks
Badges
Overall porgress

Providing visible evidence of continued learning activity.

Investigative reasoning

CY-HI emphasises the distinction between evidence and conclusions. Students are encouraged to 
consider alternative explanations and seek additional evidence before making strong claims.
Reflecting the reasoning process required in real cybersecurity investigations.
