What is CY-HI LEARN?
A cybersecurity learning platform designed to bridge the gap between university theory and
practical investigation.The platform combines structured learning, practical cybersecurity 
missions and progression systems.

Core concept
The goal is not simply to teach students how to use cybersecurity tools.CY-HI is designed
to develop investigative habits and reasoning required to understand evidence, form hypotheses,
test them and communicate conclusions.

The problem
Students can learn a large amount of theoretical knowledge at university and still not know how to
apply them to the real-world.

Common difficulties include:
Understanding cyber terminology
Knowing what to learn first
Connecting different topics together
Translating theory into practical investigation
Understanding security evidence
Developing investigative reasoning
Communicating technical findings clearly
Understanding the relationship between technical findings and business risk

The CY-HI approach
CY-HI combunes structured learning with practical investigations. Allowing students to:
Learn a cybersecurity concept
Apply the concept through a practical mission
Investigate evidence
Make and test hypotheses
Recieve feedback 
Earn XP and progress through the platform
Build investigation experience over time

Initial focus
The first major learning pathway is digital forensics. Th platform is designed so that 
additional cybersecurity areas can be included later such as:
Threat hunting
